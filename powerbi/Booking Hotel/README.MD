<h1>🏨 Hotel Booking Analysis – Power BI Project</h1> <h2>📊 Overview</h2> <p style="font-size:18px;"> This project analyzes hotel booking data using <strong>SQL</strong>, <strong>Power Query</strong>, and <strong>Power BI</strong> to deliver interactive dashboards that help understand customer behavior, hotel revenues, and cancellation patterns. </p> <h2>📂 Dataset</h2> <p style="font-size:18px;"> <strong>Source:</strong> <a href="https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/jessemostipak/hotel-booking-demand">Kaggle – Hotel Booking Demand Dataset</a><br> <strong>Rows:</strong> 191,930<br> <strong>Columns:</strong> 32<br> <strong>Observation:</strong> Most bookings came from <strong>Western European countries</strong>. </p> <h2>🛠️ Data Preparation</h2> <h3>🧹 Data Cleaning</h3> <p style="font-size:16px;"> Cleaned using <strong>Power Query</strong> (handle Nulls, adjust values, unify columns).<br> Unified <code>reserved_room_type</code> and <code>assigned_room_type</code> into one Dimension (<strong>DimRoomType</strong>) with separate Foreign Keys. </p> <h3>🗂️ Dimensions</h3> <p style="font-size:16px;"> <strong>10 Dimensions created to support the model:</strong><br> <strong>Using SQL:</strong><br> - DimRoomType: merged reserved_room_type and assigned_room_type<br> - DimCountry<br> - DimCustomer<br> - DimMeal<br> - DimMarketSegment<br> - DimDistributionChannel<br> - DimHotel<br> - DimCustomerType<br> - DimDepositType<br> <strong>Using Power Query (M Language):</strong><br> - DimDate: from Min → Max Arrival Date, including Year, Month, Quarter, WeekNumber </p> <h2>📐 DAX Measures & Calculations</h2> <p style="font-size:16px;"> <strong>Measures:</strong><br> Total Booking, Avg Lead Time, Cancelation, Avg ADR, Total Night, Cancelation Rate, Total Guest, Total Revenue (Completed), Lost Revenue<br> <strong>Calculated Columns:</strong><br> Room Status: "SAME" or "NOT SAME" (Reserved vs Assigned)<br> LeadTimeGroup: "0-7 Days", "8-30 Days", "31-90 Days", "91+ Days" </p> <h2>📑 Report Pages</h2> <p style="font-size:16px;"> The report includes 4 main pages:<br> 1. Overview – general performance summary<br> 2. Booking – detailed bookings<br> 3. Cancelation – cancellations and patterns<br> 4. Revenue – earned vs. lost revenues<br> ✨ Added Drill Through to show detailed insights per country </p> <h2>🔎 Key Insights</h2> <p style="font-size:16px;"> - Western European countries had the highest bookings<br> - City Hotels experienced a higher cancellation rate than Resort Hotels<br> - Average stay (Total Nights) was longer in Resort Hotels<br> - TA/TO (Travel Agents/Tour Operators) accounted for most bookings<br> - Transient customers were the largest customer segment<br> - Lost Revenue due to cancellations was significant in some countries<br> - Room Status Analysis: "SAME" bookings were less frequent but had a much higher cancellation rate<br> Possible reason: lack of flexibility—customers cannot change rooms easily, leading to higher cancellations </p>
